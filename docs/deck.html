02 / 11
Problem

Money stops where the signal does.

1.4Badults unbanked — most hold a phone, not a bank account
~6%average cost to send a remittance — the world's most expensive money
100s of millionson intermittent networks: rural markets, transit, blackouts, border towns

Digital dollars exist — but every wallet assumes a live connection at the moment of payment. In the places that need dollars most, that assumption breaks daily. And local currencies can lose double-digit value in a year while people wait.

03 / 11
Solution

A dollar wallet that works like a messenger — and doesn't need the network to say yes.

Sign offline

Payments are signed on-device with the user's own key. No connection needed at the moment of payment.

→

Queue & relay

Signed transactions queue locally; the mesh relay carries them hop-by-hop toward connectivity.

→

Settle on Arc

The moment any path reconnects, funds settle final in ~480 ms — fees priced in dollars, paid in USDC.

  • Chat and money in one thread — send USDC inside a conversation, like a message.
  • Self-custody — keys never leave the device. We can't touch user funds.
  • Feature phones included — a companion USSD service brings USDC to phones with no apps at all.

Gas paid in USDC

On/off-ramp built in

Plus: 9 languages · a standalone USSD service for feature phones · Pro design system · landing page live.
